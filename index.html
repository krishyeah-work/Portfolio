How I work with AI

AI makes the prototype. I make the calls.

At Intuit I used Claude to get from approved patterns to working prototypes faster, so product conversations could start from something real instead of a static mock.

  1. 01

    Ground it

    MeRead the requirements and review the existing Figma patterns with product and design, so the prototype starts from what's already approved.

    AINothing yet. The inputs are a human job.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    MeDirect the build: which pattern, which states, which edge cases matter.

    AITurns that direction into a working, clickable prototype in hours, not days.

  3. 03

    Review & iterate

    MePresent to product partners, catch what's wrong, decide what changes.

    AIApplies the changes quickly between review rounds.

  4. 04

    Hand off

    MeCheck the result against the design system and prepare designs or code for developers.

    AIDrafts implementation-ready structure that I review before it goes anywhere.

About

Hi, I'm Krishna.

I'm a UX designer drawn to the high-stakes, unglamorous parts of a product: the cart, the bill, the fee table, the plan change. If those screens are unclear, people hesitate, call support, or walk away.

I joined Cognizant in May 2025 after a Master of Design in UX at IIT Guwahati. From November 2025 to August 2026 I was part of Intuit's GTM Tech design team, working with design leads, product managers, engineers, and legal and finance partners on sales enablement, subscriptions, payment methods and pricing.

I learn new product areas fast, and I'd rather show an idea as a working prototype than describe it in a meeting. I'm looking for my next team in fintech, payments, AI or health — remote or hybrid, anywhere.

How I work with AI

AI-assisted prototyping, guided by design judgment.

At Intuit, I used Claude to turn approved patterns into working prototypes faster, so product discussions could begin with an interactive design rather than a static mock-up.

  1. 01

    Define

    MeReview requirements and existing Figma patterns with product and design partners, so every prototype starts from approved foundations.

    AINot used at this stage.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    MeDirect the build, including which patterns, states and edge cases to cover.

    AIProduces a working, interactive prototype in hours rather than days.

  3. 03

    Review and iterate

    MePresent to product partners, identify issues and decide on changes.

    AIApplies revisions quickly between review rounds.

  4. 04

    Hand off

    MeVerify the result against the design system and prepare designs or code for developers.

    AIDrafts implementation-ready structure, which I review before handoff.

About

Hi, I'm Krishna.

I'm a UX designer drawn to the parts of a product where clarity matters most: carts, bills, fee tables and plan changes. When these screens are unclear, customers hesitate, contact support or leave.

I trained as an architect at Christ University, Bengaluru, then completed a Master of Design at IIT Guwahati before joining Cognizant in 2025. From November 2025 to July 2026 I was part of Intuit's GTM Tech design team, working with design leads, product managers, engineers, and legal and finance partners on sales enablement, subscriptions, payment methods and pricing.

I learn new product areas quickly and prefer to communicate ideas through working prototypes. I'm looking for my next role in fintech, payments, AI or health, in a remote or hybrid setting.

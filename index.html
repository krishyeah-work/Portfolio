About

Hi, I'm Krishna.

I'm a UX designer drawn to the parts of a product where clarity matters most: carts, bills, fee tables and plan changes. When these screens are unclear, customers hesitate, contact support or leave.

I joined Cognizant in May 2025 after a Master of Design in UX at IIT Guwahati. From November 2025 to August 2026 I was part of Intuit's GTM Tech design team, working with design leads, product managers, engineers, and legal and finance partners on sales enablement, subscriptions, payment methods and pricing.

I learn new product areas quickly and prefer to communicate ideas through working prototypes. I'm looking for my next role in fintech, payments, AI or health, in a remote or hybrid setting.

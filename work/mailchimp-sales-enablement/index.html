01 — What is Mailchimp

An Intuit marketing platform for small businesses.

Mailchimp helps businesses reach customers through email, SMS and their own website. Restaurants, entertainment venues, non-profits and online sellers use it to send campaigns, automate follow-ups and track results.

Email Marketing is the core product. Everything else is added on top of it.

Primary product

Email Marketing

Campaigns to a contact list, e.g. “New Year sale is live, 50% off”.

Add-ons

SMS

Marketing texts to customers' phone numbers.

In scope

Transactional Email

Automatic emails like receipts and order confirmations.

In scope

Websites

Build and publish a site with Mailchimp.

Out of scope
02 — How businesses buy it today

Self-serve, on mailchimp.com.

Today a business picks a plan on Mailchimp's pricing page and checks out alone. Before designing anything, I mapped how that purchase works, because every rule here had to carry over to the sales flow.

03 — Why the sales side

Intuit already has salespeople talking to small businesses every day.

Intuit's sales agents sell QuickBooks from a sales dashboard. Mailchimp was sold separately, in its own tool with its own pricing. Bringing Mailchimp into the Intuit dashboard turns every sales conversation into a chance to sell it too.

Before

Two tools, two price lists

QuickBooks and Mailchimp sold in separate systems, with pricing managed in different places.

→
Goal

One dashboard

Agents buy Mailchimp on the customer's behalf in Intuit's sales tool, priced from one source of truth.

Who I designed for

Intuit sales agent

  • Avoid orders that fail later

06 — Final designs

Five steps, three products, one cart.

  1. 01Select offer
  2. 02Customer info
  3. 03Payment
  4. 04Review order
  5. 05Confirmation
The hardest question

How do we show SMS in the same cart as everything else when we aren't charging for it yet?

Step 1 · Select offer

Approval-based products get their own place in the cart.

SMS sits under an “Upon Application Approval” label with its real price and discount, outside the subtotal paid today. A tooltip tells the agent exactly when it's charged.

Step 1 · Guardrail

Stop invalid orders before they happen.

Add-ons can't be bought alone. If an agent tries to add SMS first, the message says exactly what to do instead.

Step 3 · Payment

“Total due today” means exactly that.

The cart follows the agent through checkout. Today's subtotal and tax cover only what's charged now. SMS stays visible, clearly marked for later.

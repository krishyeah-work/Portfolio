TEDx IIT Guwahati

This identity design for TEDxIITGuwahati’s 2025 edition embodies the tension between human creativity and the transient nature of our endeavors.

Overview

The beauty of creation in the face of impermanence.

The event

TEDxIITGuwahati, launched in 2016, is an annual event organized by IIT Guwahati students. Run by passionate volunteers, it offers a TED-like experience, fostering dialogue and collaboration through engaging talks and interactive sessions for IIT Guwahati and wider Indian communities.

01 — Concept & theme

Seaside Citadel

‘Seaside Citadel’ reflects the transient nature of human efforts. Like sandcastles reshaped by waves, our creations evolve and inspire new ideas. This identity captures the essence of curiosity (sun), creation (citadel), and transformation (waves).

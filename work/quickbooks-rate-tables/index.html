Case study 02 · Intuit × QuickBooks

QuickBooks Payments and Bill Pay Rate Tables

Payment fees change with the payment method, the network and the type of transaction. I designed rate tables for QuickBooks Payments and QuickBooks Bill Pay so that sales agents and customers can quickly find the charge that applies to them.

01 — Problem

Two products, two audiences and many pricing variables.

A QuickBooks Payments fee can depend on how a customer pays, which card network it runs on, what kind of transaction it is, whether the charge is range-based, and which offers apply. Bill Pay fees depend on how a bill is paid and how the vendor receives the payment.

Sales agents need to answer pricing questions quickly and accurately during a call, while customers need to find the same answer on their own.

Case study 02 · Intuit × QuickBooks

Find the fee that applies to you.

Payment fees change with the payment method, the network and the type of transaction. I designed rate tables for QuickBooks Payments and QuickBooks Bill Pay so sales agents and customers can find the charge that applies to them without decoding a pricing page.

01 — Problem

Two products, two audiences, a lot of variables.

A QuickBooks Payments fee can depend on how a customer pays, which card network it runs on, what kind of transaction it is, whether the charge is range-based, and which offers apply. Bill Pay has its own logic: how a bill is paid and how the vendor receives it.

Sales agents need to answer “what will this cost me?” quickly and correctly on a call. Customers need to answer it on their own. Same data, different jobs.

01 — The challenge

Enterprises pay by invoice. IES billed a card.

Intuit already offered Net Terms on an older billing system. IES runs on newer billing that could only charge a card or bank account.

How their finance teams pay
  1. Purchase order
  2. Invoice
  3. Approval
  4. Payment run, within terms
How IES billed them
  1. Card or bank account on file
  2. Charged on the billing date
  3. A failed charge pauses access
$1.50

One enterprise customer paying $29K a year lost access over a failed $1.50 charge.

Key design challenge

How can IES customers pay by invoice without changing checkout for anyone else?

03 — Requirements

A payment method inside a shared widget.

11
Constraint

Products and flows share one payment widget.

Used from TurboTax to the Partner Portal. Net Terms had to show only for eligible customers, and change nothing for others.

Two modes

Terms change in billing settings, never mid-purchase.

Manage mode

Billing settings

  • Apply for Net Terms
  • Track the application
  • Switch between Immediate and Net Terms
Transactional mode

Upgrade, downgrade, resubscribe, new orders

  • Pay with the terms on file
  • No applying
  • No switching terms
  • ExceptionOpen Net Terms invoices block a switch back to Immediate.
  • ExceptionIn collections, terms are locked. Only the payment method within them can change.

Decision 1

A declined application leads back to Immediate, not to Customer Care

Chose
One next step in the widget: continue with Immediate Terms.
Over
Pointing declined customers to Intuit Customer Care.
Why
Intuit doesn't explain declines, so Care couldn't help. Sending customers there would only add support calls.
After approval

The invoice becomes the payment method.

Once saved, the billing page shows Invoice Net 30 in place of the card.

Buying on terms

Decision 2

One consent sentence for cards and invoices, agreed with Legal

Chose
“By selecting [button], you authorize us to automatically charge/bill you … If you have an underlying contract specifying payment terms, we will charge/bill you in accordance with those terms.”
Over
A Net Terms version: “I authorize Intuit to invoice my account on mm/dd/yyyy and monthly after that … under the agreed Net Terms.”
Why
“Charge/bill” covers both ways to pay and defers to the customer's contract, so one sentence works in every flow.

05 — Reflection

Team, and what I learned.

Team
  • My contributionFlows for accountants, customers and partners: widget states, application, purchases, billing, invoices and emails; prototypes and handoff
  • Senior designerDesign direction and reviews
  • Product managerRequirements, scope and alignment with finance and legal
  • EngineeringWidget states, billing changes and integrations
What I learned

  • Design the in-between

    Review, decline and blocked states needed as much care as approval.

  • Rules before screens

    Agreeing when terms can change settled questions across every flow at once.

  • Copy is shared work

    Consent wording went through Legal, so words were reviewed as closely as layouts.
